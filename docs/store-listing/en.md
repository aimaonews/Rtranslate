Read foreign-language pages as naturally as your native tongue. Rtranslate is an open-source bilingual translation extension: original and result stay side by side on the page, with layout and links intact. Free engines work out of the box, no account needed.

✨ KEY FEATURES

✅ Bilingual page view — original and result shown paragraph by paragraph, only when visible; one click restores the original
✅ Multiple engines — a free Google/Microsoft/MyMemory chain with failover, plus DeepL, OpenAI-compatible endpoints, self-hosted LibreTranslate and custom APIs
✅ Document translation — PDF, EPUB, DOCX, HTML, Markdown, TXT and SRT/VTT/ASS subtitles, exported side by side or alone
✅ Video subtitles — bilingual YouTube subtitles with smart merging, plus live captions for online meetings
✅ Privacy masking — emails, phone numbers, bank cards, ID numbers and API keys are masked on your device before text is sent out, then restored locally
✅ AI experts and terminology — custom prompt strategies, built-in domain glossaries, smart term extraction for consistent wording
✅ Wordbook and flashcards — save words with sentence context, review with FSRS spaced repetition, see them highlighted on pages
✅ Alt+D comparison — press Alt+D to compare several engines side by side

🚀 HOW IT WORKS

1. Add Rtranslate and pin it to the toolbar — free engines start working immediately.
2. Click the icon or press Alt+T to translate a page, or select any text for the bubble popup.
3. Open Settings when you want more: add your own API key, pick an AI expert, or turn on masking.

🔒 PRIVACY

No account, no telemetry, no middle servers. Settings and API keys stay in the browser's local storage. With masking on, sensitive data never leaves your device unmasked — placeholders go out, the result is restored locally.
